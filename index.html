How this would work for real

Nothing on this page is exotic. It's the standard 2026 small-business stack, and every piece below has a free tier or a pay-as-you-go price.

Accounts

Magic-link sign-in (type your email, click the link, done) via Supabase or Firebase Auth. Your profile and dogs live in one row each. You never retype them.

Membership billing

Stripe Billing runs the $250 or $375 monthly subscription. Stripe Checkout hosts the card form, so card numbers never touch the site, and the Stripe Customer Portal handles upgrades, card changes and cancellations.

Booking

A bookings table with a 6-dog cap per class, enforced in the database. Credits reset on each billing cycle, and a webhook from Stripe keeps membership status in sync.

Hosting

This page is static HTML on GitHub Pages, which is free. Confirmation emails and calendar invites go out through a transactional email service such as Resend or Postmark.

Honest footnote (for the trainers reading this)

Building your own scheduler isn't free. Someone has to maintain it, and card processing costs about the same whether Acuity or your own site sends the charge to Stripe. What you'd really gain is members who book more classes because booking takes ten seconds. Before going custom, it's worth checking whether your current tool has a client-login or "remember my info" setting you can turn on, or looking at membership-first tools such as Gingr, PocketSuite or Mindbody-style platforms, which do this out of the box.

Either way, we love the classes. That's why we keep booking them. 🐾
